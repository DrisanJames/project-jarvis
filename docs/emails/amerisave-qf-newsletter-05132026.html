💡 Did You Know?

🚀 Astronauts grow up to 2 inches taller in space. Without gravity compressing the spine, the vertebral discs expand. Astronauts return to their normal height within a few weeks of returning to Earth.

🚶 The average person walks five times around the Earth in a lifetime. That’s roughly 100,000 miles over an average lifespan. Most of us take between 7,500 and 10,000 steps daily without thinking about it.

🦄 Scotland’s national animal is the unicorn. It has been a Scottish heraldic symbol since the 12th century. In Celtic mythology, the unicorn represented purity, innocence, and power.
